How it works

Four simple steps to exam-ready.

  1. 1

    Add inputs

    Enter days until exam, hours per day, and build subjects with topics and subtopics. Set priority weights per subject.

  2. 2

    Generate schedule

    The planner distributes time across subtopics based on priority. Revision and break slots are inserted automatically.

  3. 3

    Review, track, and practice

    See a day-wise timeline. Mark sessions done, use the Pomodoro timer, take AI quizzes, and add personal notes.

  4. 4

    Save, export, and revisit

    Save plans locally, export to PDF/CSV/.ics, and revisit from the Saved Plans section anytime.
